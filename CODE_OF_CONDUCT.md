# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 3.0][code] --
that page is canonical. Summary for convenience:

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this community a harassment-free
experience for everyone, and to act in ways that contribute to an open,
welcoming, and respectful community.

## Standards

Be respectful, welcome differing viewpoints, and give/accept feedback
gracefully. Harassment, insults, personal attacks, and publishing others'
private information without consent are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report abusive or unacceptable behavior to <suriyawa@tcd.ie>. Reports will
be reviewed promptly, and the reporter's privacy will be respected.

[code]: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/3/0/code_of_conduct/
